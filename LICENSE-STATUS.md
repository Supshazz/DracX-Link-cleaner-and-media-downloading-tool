# License Status

This document describes the licensing boundary for the DracX project. It is not a replacement for the license text distributed with any third-party component.

## DracX project materials

The repository owner has not yet selected and published a single top-level open-source license for all original DracX materials. Until a license is added at the repository root, do not assume that the source is licensed for unrestricted reuse, redistribution or commercial use.

## Third-party components

DracX integrates third-party software. Each dependency, subproject, asset and bundled runtime remains subject to its own license and applicable terms. Preserve the original license and notice files and review the terms before redistributing a modified bundle.

In particular, the BgUtils component has its own GPL-3.0-only licensing terms. Other bundled dependencies may have different licenses.

## Artwork and trademarks

The DracX icon/background artwork and third-party product/platform names may have separate ownership and usage restrictions. A public repository does not grant rights to third-party artwork or trademarks.

## Before changing licensing

The repository owner should review all original and bundled materials, confirm redistribution rights for artwork, and select an appropriate project-level license with legal advice where needed.
